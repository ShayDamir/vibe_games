DER·DIE·DAS

The German Gender Shooter

  • 01Words fly at you. Shoot the matching gender before the word slips past: 1 DER (blue), 2 DIE (pink), 3 DAS (yellow) — or tap the gun pads.
  • 02Correct shot = 1 coin + streak bonus. Every 5-streak raises the bonus and the speed — words fly in faster. Wrong gender = streak (and speed) lost. Letting a word fly past costs nothing.
  • 03After every word you see its translation and the gender rule behind it. Press SPACE or ❚❚ any time to pause and read it slowly. Red chips are exceptions — memorize those!
  • 04Between rounds, spend coins in the Rule Shop to unlock new rules — each one adds new words to the pool. The shop offers up to 2 fresh rules per gender, picked at random.
  • 05The shop also unlocks word levels — a2, b1, b2… — each one adds its words to the pool. Words you got wrong come back with an ERROR RECOVERY label: nail them for double coins.

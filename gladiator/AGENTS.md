# The Last Gladiator

First-person, turn-based gladiator combat: you and the foe act simultaneously each round (Attack / Dodge / Defend / Cast), you farm coins to buy steel, armor, shields, scrolls and training, and you hunt 12 hidden combos ("arcana") that only reveal themselves when you pull them off. Built for a 10-year-old chess fan: readable at a glance, deep under the surface.

## How the game works

- **Rounds are simultaneous.** Each round both fighters commit at once; if both attack, both take damage. A battle ends by KO or after `MAX_ROUND` (25) rounds (draw — no gold).
- **Seedable battles** (`?seed=`): the query param seeds the global `rnd` (mulberry32 in `data.js`). `game.js` re-seeds at the start of *every* battle, so a given seed replays identically no matter what came before — useful for debugging and a future daily-arena challenge. A 🎲 badge in the top HUD shows the active seed.
- **Battle log** keeps the last 46 lines. A line carrying a bold token (`<b>`) is *pinned* — damage, combos, shield breaks, events — and never collapses. When more than 10 non-bold lines (defends, dodges, rests, tells) pile up between pinned lines, the oldest folds into a single `…` (`state.logLoose` + `foldOldLoose` in `game.js`), so the log stays focused on what mattered. On the result screen the log floats above the overlay (`#log.over-result`, set by `showScreen`) so the finished battle stays readable; it's cleared at the next `startBattle`.
- **The sand rewinds (time-rewind).** On a defeat or a draw, shield durability and consumed scrolls are restored to their pre-battle state (`state.shieldSnapshot`/`scrollSnapshot` in `game.js`); only a victory consumes consumables. Losing is free — grinding a level has no economic penalty.
- **Actions.**
  - **Attack** — weapon damage with ±10% variance. Fists (tier 0) never chip shields.
  - **Dodge** — an *action*, not a passive stat. Every fighter has a small **passive** evasion (player: 15 + 4/footwork rank + titles + armor modifier; foes: 8–20 by archetype + armor) that rolls against every attack, and choosing **Dodge** this round adds **+55** to that roll (≈70–95% to slip an incoming attack, at the cost of doing nothing). Frozen/rooted fighters cannot dodge at all.
  - **Defend** — with a shield: full block, 0 damage, the strike chips 1 use (fists don't); at 0 uses the shield shatters. Without a shield: parry for half damage.
  - **Cast** — spend a scroll (magic ignores armor). Scrolls: fire (18), water (6 + stun + soaked), earth (10 + stun), wind (8 + foe's next attack −25%), lightning (26), ice (10 + frozen), life (heal 22), shadow (foe's next physical attack misses).
  - **Chant** (Chanting training, once per battle, key `Q`) — a free cast slot: resolve a scroll, then still take the normal action in the same round (`game.js` resolves it between `askPlayer` and `enemyChoose`; costs 18 stamina, never usable while stunned).
- **State machine** (per fighter, in `combat.js`): `stun` (skips next action; ticks at round start), `frozen` (2 rounds: no dodge, own attack −50% consumed once, heavy hits ×2 consumed), `soaked` (2 rounds, fuel for Conductor), `wind` (next attack −25%, consumed on attempt), `shadow` (next physical attack misses, consumed on attempt), `rooted` (2 rounds, no dodge), `ward` (2 rounds, +30% dodge — mage self-buff), `aegis` (2 rounds, +25% dodge — granted by every successful block with the Tortoise Aegis), `feather` (next attack +25% — set when you dodge an enemy spell, consumed on the next attack attempt), `burn`/`poison` (tick at round end).
- **Hidden combos** (the "arcana"; never listed in the shop — the Arcana page shows ❓ until discovered, then the name + how):
  - **Ice Shatter** — heavy weapon (tier 4+) vs a frozen foe → double damage.
  - **Conductor** — lightning vs a soaked foe → double damage + stun.
  - **Dragonfire** — Flamebrand + Dragon Hide → physical strikes ignore armor.
  - **Warded Block** — Enchanted Robe + shield vs an enemy spell → reflect half the magic back at the caster.
  - **Counter Jab** — dodge an attack from a wind- or frost-weakened foe → 5 counter damage.
  - **Battering Ram** — block with a Tower Shield → 8 recoil damage to the attacker.
  - **Boiling Venom** — fire a poisoned foe → poison doubles for its remaining rounds.
  - **Iron Composure** — Iron Plate vs an enemy earthquake → 0 damage, the stun still lands.
  - **Cauterize** — fire a stunned foe → it also burns (4/round ×3).
  - **Featherfall** — dodge an enemy spell → your next attack deals +25% damage.
  - **Mirror Ward** — Enchanted Robe + Tower Shield vs an enemy spell → reflect the *full* spell back (upgrades Warded Block's half-reflect).
  - **Heart of the Sand** — win a battle you were below 25% HP in at some point → +50% coins (battle-level combo, tracked on `state.lowHpEver`, applied after title multipliers but before last-stand halving).
- **Enemies** scale with your win count (`level = wins + 1`): HP `50 + level*10`, damage `weapon.dmg * (1 + level*0.02)` (common foes only — the weapon ladder in `commonWeaponFor` carries the scaling). Five archetypes with reactive AI. **Each round the foe pre-commits** his plan (`combat.foeCommit()`, called right after `beginRound`) from what he can see — your stamina and your recent actions, *never* your hidden current pick (that's what keeps rounds simultaneous) — and a brief colored **windup telegraph** (`Scene.foeTelegraph`) flashes that intent at round start so a sharp eye can read the habits (chess depth, less luck). The glow is a short pulse (~1.1 s, fades — not a permanent status), and **tricksters never telegraph** (the unreadable one). The AI itself is unchanged by the telegraph — it's pure information for the player. **Brute** (mostly attacks), **Duelist** (fast; presses harder after you dodge), **Arena Mage** (innate fire/earth/ward/life spells on a 2-round cooldown — `spellCd` ticks down at round end in `tickDots`; one round before a recast he shows a ✨ "gathers power" tell in the log + a pulsing status icon; earth stuns you, that's the only player-derailing effect), **Wall** (shields; reads your attacks and braces after two), **Trickster** (level 8+, weighted 2; mostly weighted-random actions with an occasional flash of insight — deliberately unreadable; white mask in `scene.js`, 22 passive dodge, no shield). Every 5th foe is a **Champion**: the legendary for its weapon slot (the legendary is its level, so champions skip the damage ramp), flat ×1.05 HP, +5% passive dodge, non-wall champions may roll Dragon Hide (35%), and **Rage** below 30% HP (+30% damage). Tuned so champion levels sit at ~35–45% for a well-prepared player. Tricksters are re-rolled to a non-trickster archetype when the level is a champion level.
- **Last Stand** (`game.js`): on a defeat the result screen offers one final fight — a fresh foe of the same level at 50% starting HP, worth half the reward (`state.lastStand`). No last stand after a lost last stand; a lost last stand still rewinds consumables like any defeat.
  - **Arena moods**: every battle sets a mood by level — dusk (default), **night** (level % 5 === 0), **blood-moon** (level % 10 === 0). `Scene.setMood(mood)` in `scene.js` regenerates the sky gradient, swaps the sun sprite for the moon, and retunes fog/ambient/key/fill/torch lighting from the `MOODS` table; `game.js` picks the mood in `startBattle` and logs a line for night/blood-moon.
  - **Player death cam** (`Scene.playerDie()` in `scene.js`, called from `endBattle('lose')`): on a defeat the first-person camera sinks to the sand and tips over, ending staring at the arena sky; the lights gutter out and a `#blackout` DOM overlay (z 45, under the result screen) fades the frame to black over ~2.5 s. `endBattle` then delays the DEFEAT screen 2.6 s so it appears on top of the dark. `sceneStart()` resets the cam, the hands and the overlay for the next fight.
- **Arena events** (35% of battles, announced at the start): Bloodlust (first attack each round +30%), Whetstones (your first attack of the battle +50%), Crowd Favorite (coins +25%), Cracked Walls (every shield chip costs +1).
- **Economy.** Win reward `24 + 9*level + 2*(streak−1)`, then title multipliers and Crowd Favorite. Shop tabs: Weapons (7 common + 6 legendary, equipped instantly), Armor (4) & Shields (consumables: wooden 3 / iron 5 / tower 4 uses, durability persists between battles and rewinds on loss — see above; plus the legendary **Tortoise Aegis** 🐢, 6 uses, whose every successful block grants +25% dodge for 2 rounds), Scrolls (buy into a satchel), Training (Conditioning +10 HP/rank ×10, Footwork +4% dodge/rank ×3, Second Wind: once per battle, first drop below 25% HP restores 25, Chanting 200: once per battle, a free cast slot — chant a scroll and still take your action), Relics (one-off keepsakes bought once that act on their own: **Phial of the Healer** 🧪 300 — once per battle, the first drop below 30% HP auto-restores 30), Arcana (discovery codex).
- **Titles** are auto-earned by wins (3/5/8/12/16): max HP, coin %, dodge %, damage % bonuses; shown in the HUD and on the hub.
- **Progress** (coins, gear, satchel, shield durability, wins/losses/draws/streaks, discovered combos, training, mute, crowd volume (`crowdVol`, 0..1 — the crowd slider), intro-seen) persists in localStorage under `last_gladiator_save_v1`.

## Module APIs (contracts)

- `combat` (global object): `me` / `foe` getters; `setFoe(f)`, `reset()` (rebuilds `me` from `save`), `makeEnemy(level)`, `foeCommit()` → `{type, spell?}` (the foe pre-commits at round start from visible state; call it right after `beginRound`, then flash the result as a telegraph), `enemyChoose(playerActionType)` → `{type, spell?}` (records the player's action and returns the already-committed `foe.committed`), `resolveAttack(att, def, ctx)` / `resolveSpell(caster, target, element)` / `resolveFoeSpell(caster, target, spell)` → result `{dmg, heal, dodged, blocked, parried, miss, shieldHit, shieldBroken, combos[], procs[], counters[]}`, `tickDots()` → dot results, `beginRound()`. `ctx = {round, event, firstStriker, whetUsed}` — `whetUsed` is mutated by the engine.
- `Scene` (global object): `initScene()`, `sceneStart()` (walk-in + camera fly, returns a Promise), `playerDie()` (defeat death cam, returns a Promise that resolves when the frame is fully dark), `rebuildEnemyVisual()` (reads `combat.foe`), `buildPlayerView()` (reads `save.equipped`), `enemyPos()`, `setMood('dusk'|'night'|'bloodmoon')`, `foeTelegraph('attack'|'defend'|'dodge'|'cast'|'none')` (brief windup glow of the foe's committed intent; auto-fades ~1.1 s), `eAnim.{attack,hurt,defend,dodge,die,cast,stun}`, `handAttack('slash'|'stab'|'swing'|'cast')`, `fxSwingStreak(kind)` (Promise resolving at contact, plays the swoosh), other `fx*` (some return Promises that resolve at the impact moment and play their own impact SFX — see the code comments; `game.js` only adds SFX the scene doesn't own), `floatWorld/floatMe`, `crowdRoar`, `triggerSlowmo`.
- `Sfx`: `unlock()`, then one method per sound (`click, coin, buy, deny, swing, hit, hitHeavy, block, crack, breakShield, dodge, shatter, cast, fire, water, earth, wind, lightning, ice, heal, shadow, stun, rage, horn, roar, victory, defeat, draw, discover, title`). `Ambience.start()/setBattle(bool)/stop()/setCrowdVolume(0..1)` (live-scales the crowd murmur only — drums untouched; `Sfx.roar` also scales by `save.crowdVol` and is skipped at 0).

## Conventions

- No dependencies, no build step, no test framework. Plain `<script>` tags, globals, `var`-style code (the repo stays ES5-ish).
- Content lives in `data.js`; mechanics in `combat.js`; visuals in `scene.js`; flow in `game.js`. Don't blur those boundaries.
- `combat.js` must stay free of DOM/three.js so it can be exercised headlessly (see below).
- Shields are consumables: `save.shieldDur[key]` is the source of truth, synced back from the fighter at battle end; a shattered shield is auto-unequipped before the next fight.
- New scroll/weapon/armor/shield/combo/relic: add it to `data.js` first, then wire resolution in `combat.js`, animation in `game.js` (`playPlayerSpell`/`playProc`/`weaponAnimKind`), and it will appear in the shop automatically (objects are iterated) — scroll/arcana/relic pages iterate `SCROLL_ORDER`/`COMBO_ORDER`/`RELIC_ORDER`, so add the key there too. Relics are save-flag items (`save.relics[]`), triggered in the `runRounds` loop (`state.flaskUsed` pattern) — they are not consumables, so the time-rewind never touches them.

## No test framework

- Syntax: `bun build --no-bundle gladiator/<file>.js` (node isn't available on this machine; `bun` is).
- Engine sim: concat `data.js` + `combat.js` into a scratch script with a `localStorage` stub and run thousands of random fights (guards against NaN/regressions — the reference sim lives in the session history, not the repo).
- Headless smoke test of the full flow: `bun gladiator/tools/smoke.js` — stubs `document`/`window`/`THREE`/`Sfx`/`Ambience`/`Scene`, evals `data.js` + `combat.js` + `game.js`, boots, then plays real battles through the captured keydown handler (at 50× timer speed, ~1 s per battle) and asserts on reward math, chant, last stand, mage cooldown, trickster, arena mood and seed determinism. Run it after touching `game.js`/`combat.js`/`data.js`.
- The real check is still opening `gladiator/index.html` in a browser (internet required for the three.js + font CDNs). With no internet, the `#cdn-error` overlay explains why and offers a reload, instead of a dead canvas.

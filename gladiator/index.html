THE LAST GLADIATOR
You bought your way out of the mines. Now you fight in the sand.
  • ⚔️ Turn by turn: every round you and your foe act at the same time. Attack, Dodge, Defend, Cast a scroll — or Rest.
  • 🌬️ Stamina is truth: every action spends stamina, and you can see your foe's. When his bar runs low, his next move is written on his face — heavy arms are slow, fists are not.
  • 🪙 Earn coins, buy better steel, train your body. Shields shatter — they are consumables. But losing rewinds the sand: a lost battle returns your shield and scrolls whole. Only wins consume.
  • 🔮 The arena keeps secrets: hidden combos between magic, steel and armor. Find them, and they become your edge.
  • 👑 Every fifth foe is a Champion — stronger, legendary, and capable of rage. Beat them to climb.
